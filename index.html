About this prototype

StudySpace shows students where they can study before they have to search.

What is real

  • The map is the real MazeMap map of BI (campus 10), loaded from MazeMap.
  • The building shape follows public descriptions: four blocks split by two cross-shaped internal streets, with the library on floors 4 and 5.
  • The occupancy rules follow the concept: button, sensor backup and a 30-minute automatic release.

What is simulated

  • All occupancy is generated by simulated students. No sensors are connected.
  • Bookings and sharing stay on this device and clear when you reload the page.
  • Friends are samples. A link you send shows a fixed spot, not a live position.
  • Seat, table and room positions are placeholders drawn on top of the real map.
  • MazeMap integration is a proposed partnership, not an agreement.
